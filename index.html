FAQ

Questions about the apps

Can the apps do everything the web platform does?

Yes. The Windows and Android apps have the same capabilities as Think4Ever on the web.

How much do the apps cost?

The apps are free to download, with in-app purchases for paid plans. Your Think4Ever plan sets your projects, users and credits. See pricing.

Can I use my existing Think4Ever account?

Yes. Sign in with the same account you use on think4ever.com.
